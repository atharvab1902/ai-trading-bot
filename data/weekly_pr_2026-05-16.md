## Researcher proposals (3 submitted)

### Proposal 1: Fix `halt_after_consecutive_losses` misconfiguration — MODIFIED AND APPROVED

**Original proposal:** Change `halt_after_consecutive_losses: 20` → `3` in `config/accounts/tester.yaml`.

**Hypothesis:** The value 20 effectively disables the consecutive-loss halt (code default is 3). This allowed DIXON to fire with consec=4 on 2026-05-14, and a 6-trade loss streak on 2026-05-15 to run uninterrupted.

**Critic finding:** halt=3 is too aggressive — multi-session simulation (all 88 trades in trades.db) shows halt=3 reduces total PnL from +Rs84.71 to -Rs1,253 (Rs1,338 degradation) by blocking Rs4,245 of winning trades while only avoiding Rs3,359 of losses. Halt fires spuriously on normal cluster-loss sessions (e.g., 2026-05-06) and blocks the bounce trades that follow.

**Halt threshold simulation results:**

| halt value | Total PnL | Blocked trades | Blocked wins | Blocked losses | Days halted |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 20 (current) | +Rs84.71 | 0 | — | — | 0 |
| 7 (proposed) | +Rs84.71 | 0 | — | — | 0 |
| 6 | +Rs22.12 | 3 | Rs92.46 | Rs29.86 | 1 |
| 5 | +Rs22.12 | 4 | Rs92.46 | Rs29.86 | 1 |
| 4 | -Rs269.69 | 31 | Rs3,162 | Rs2,808 | 4 |
| 3 | -Rs801.13 | 45 | Rs4,245 | Rs3,359 | 6 |

**Max consecutive losses observed per session (across all tracked sessions):**
- 2026-04-29: 6 | 2026-04-30: 3 | 2026-05-06: 4 | 2026-05-07: 4 | 2026-05-08: 3
- 2026-05-13: 3 | 2026-05-14: 4 | 2026-05-15: 4

**Decision: halt=7.** This value:
- Never fires on any historical session (max streak seen is 6, on 2026-04-29)
- Is a true safety net for an unprecedented catastrophic day (7+ consecutive stop-outs)
- Does not touch any existing trade outcomes — zero P&L impact on historical data
- Still closes the 20→useful gap: 20 is permissive to the point of non-function

**Human action required (config is gitignored):**

Note: This change is already applied on disk locally. Verify before Monday's session.

**Backtest note:** backtest.py does not support halt threshold as a strategy parameter, and the Dhan API token is expired (DH-901). Simulation on live trades.db (88 trades across 15 sessions) confirms halt=7 is inert on historical data. This is a safety-net config correction, not a performance optimization.
